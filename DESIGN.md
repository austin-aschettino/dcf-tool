# Design System: Stock & Fund Valuation

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "The Columnar Pad"**

The app is an accountant's green analysis pad. A valuation is arithmetic that foots, so every page shows its working down to a double-ruled answer: lines of figures right-aligned to one shared column, an operator in the margin, a subtotal in heavier ink, and a total underlined twice with today's price written beneath it. The pad is bound in ledger-green bookcloth (the sidebar), its pages are pale green stock, and the ink is near-black, with red ink for negatives and one blue ink set aside for the visitor's own assumptions.

Density is a working sheet's, not a brochure's: flat sheets with hairline rules, no shadows, no tinted gradient cards or banners, no star scores, no brand-indigo everywhere. Words are set in Public Sans; every numeral, at every size and inside running sentences, is set in Source Serif 4 with tabular lining figures, so a number always looks like a number and columns never shift when a slider moves. The verdict is written in plain words first, anchored on one figure and coloured only in its headline ink, and only when the price is well away from that figure; the arithmetic sits beside it as a footing, on the stock page and the fund page alike; the methods chart follows on the Overview tab.

Dark mode is the same pad at night: a deep green-black ground, the same hairlines lifted, the inks brightened to hold contrast. Nothing changes structurally between themes.

**Key Characteristics:**
- Ledger-green bookcloth sidebar on a pale green pad ground.
- One serif figure face (Source Serif 4, tabular lining) for every number, layered in front of Public Sans.
- Blue ink reserved for the visitor's own assumptions and what is computed from them; what the market prices in is written in ledger green.
- Red ink and accounting parentheses for negatives, in footings and data tables alike.
- One verdict colour rule everywhere: plain ink within 15% of the estimate, red well above, green well below.
- Flat sheets, 1px hairline rules, 4/6/8px corners, no shadows.
- The footing (a column of figures ending on a double-ruled total) as the signature for computed answers.

## Colors

A green-on-green paper palette of inks, where every hue beyond the greens means something specific.

### Primary
- **Ledger Bookcloth Green** (ledger-green): the sidebar cloth, Streamlit's primary colour, the active tab underline and label, the "Home" crumb, expander headings, step numerals on Home, the phone-only sidebar hint. Structure and navigation, never a judgement.
- **Raised Cloth** (ledger-green-raised): the selected sidebar menu item, and the primary button fill in dark mode.
- **Deep Cloth** (ledger-cloth-deep) and **Cloth Rule** (cloth-rule): the sidebar's secondary surfaces and the hairline under the brand.
- **Cloth Ink** (cloth-ink) and **Cloth Caption** (cloth-caption): text on the sidebar; captions are set to about 7:1 on the cloth rather than faded.

### Secondary
- **The Visitor's Blue Ink** (you-blue): the visitor's own numbers wherever they are quoted in text (the `.you` style, bold), including the phone-only "Your assumptions" line above the tabs; the cash-flow model's bar on the fair-value chart (the model runs on the visitor's settings), the "Middle (your settings)" scenario, "Your assumption" and "Your forecast" bars and lines beside the market's priced-in growth, the forecast bars of cash-flow and fund charts, the outline of the current cell in what-if grids, links, the text-selection tint and keyboard focus rings. **Blue on the Cloth** (you-blue-on-cloth) is the same ink lifted for the sidebar: slider thumb values, the sidebar's primary colour, and the fill of the tickers the visitor has picked, which are written in **Deep Blue** (you-blue-deep) on it (about 8:1). ### Tertiary
- **Red Ink** (red-ink): negatives (in parentheses) and "priced well above the estimate" verdicts and demand levels.
- **Cheap Green** (cheap-green): "priced well below the estimate" verdicts and demand levels, and Strong badges.

- **Badge tints** (badge-strong-tint / -edge, badge-ok-tint / -ink / -edge, badge-weak-tint / -ink / -edge): the quality scorecard's pale grade tags only. Badge OK ink is a deeper amber than amber-ink and Badge Weak ink a deeper red than red-ink, so each holds contrast on its tint.
- **Chip edges** (chip-ledger-edge, chip-you-edge): the borders of the ledger and "you" title chips.
- **Portfolio mix:** the sector pie uses a ledger palette in fixed order: #1e4636, #4f8a6c, #a8761b, #5b4a8a, #2f7f8f, #8a978f, #c9b27a, #b9c9bf.

### Neutral

- **Rule** (rule): panel edges, the tab baseline, zero lines, the pad note's two rules. **Hairline** (hairline): footing row rules and dividers between figures in a row. **Chart Grid** (chart-grid): Plotly gridlines.
- **Ink** (ink): headings, figures, today's-price line, and every verdict headline that sits within the fair band. **Soft Ink** (ink-soft): body copy, chart text, pad notes and the "Your assumptions" line's words. **Muted Ink** (muted-ink): labels, captions (main column), table headers, inactive tabs, disabled buttons, footing operators, chart range labels and the footer.
- **Reference lines** are ink: today's price on the fair-value chart, "today's money" forecast lines, and the S&P 500 benchmark (dotted). "All stocks" is muted ink (dashed).
- Night counterparts: night-ground, night-sheet, night-rule, night-hairline, night-ink, night-muted, night-ledger, night-cloth (the sidebar at night, set in the dark stylesheet), the lifted inks night-you-blue, night-red-ink, night-cheap-green, night-amber, and the chart inks night-peer-green, night-analyst-amber, night-market-grey. **Night Heading** (night-heading) lifts headings and metric values one step above night ink. Up and down deltas and the Strong / Weak badges share one pair of night tints: **Night Up** (night-up-ink on night-up-tint, edged night-up-edge) and **Night Down** (night-down-ink on night-down-tint, edged night-down-edge); the night OK badge is night-amber on its own 14% wash. At night the what-if and priced-in grids tint cells with **night-grid-up** and **night-grid-down**, stronger washes than the badges so a tinted cell still reads as green or red on the dark sheet.

### Named Rules
**The Blue Ink Rule.** Blue means "yours". It marks only the visitor's assumptions, choices and figures computed from them; what the market's price implies is ledger green, analysts are amber, and market data, peers and the verdict never use blue. Nothing decorative is blue.

**The Red Ink Rule.** Negatives are written in red ink inside accounting parentheses, with the closing bracket hanging outside the figure column so digits stay aligned. Red and green are kept for value against price and never used for categories.

**The Fifteen Percent Rule.** Every verdict on the site, the price against the estimate and the growth priced in against its yardstick alike, uses one rule: within 15% of the estimate it is plain ink with no colour; well above, red ink; well below, cheap green. "In line" and "can't work out" are neutral, never amber. The same rule inks every "estimate vs. price" gap figure: the Gap column on Compare and the "Gap vs. model" column on My portfolio (red ink or cheap green, 600, plain within the band), and the bars of Compare's "Estimate vs. price" chart (peer green, red ink, or market grey within the band). Grid cells take the day inks in both themes, because at night the data grids are drawn light and inverted. The backtest's verdict follows it too: an inconclusive result ("No clear difference") is plain ink, and its difference between two returns is written in points.

**The Bookcloth Rule.** Ledger green binds and navigates (sidebar, active tab, crumbs, expander headings). It does not carry a verdict; verdicts, and the demand levels that echo them, use cheap-green and red-ink, or plain ink.

**The Source Ink Rule.** On a chart, a colour names whose number it is: blue for the visitor, ledger green for the market's price, amber for analysts, grey for the past, yardsticks and context, ink for reference lines. Amber never marks today's price.

## Typography

**Display Font:** Public Sans (with -apple-system, Segoe UI, sans-serif)
**Body Font:** Public Sans
**Figure Font:** Source Serif 4 (with Georgia, Times New Roman, serif), tabular lining numerals

**Character:** A plain, public-service grotesque for the words and an old-style-born serif for the numbers, so a reader can tell at a glance which marks are arithmetic. Headings are 700 with a slight negative track (-0.01em).

Figures reach every surface two ways: a `--figures` stack applied directly to metric values, footing values, quality-card values and step numerals; and a numerals-only web face, **Pad Figures**, cut from Source Serif 4 with a unicode-range of digits, `$ % ( ) + .` and the true minus, placed before Public Sans in every text stack (including Plotly's font family and every table). Commas and hyphens are left out of the range so words keep Public Sans punctuation. The whole app sets `font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums lining-nums`. Data grids draw on a canvas that ignores page CSS, so the theme's body font in `.streamlit/config.toml` is itself the stack `Pad Figures, Public Sans, sans-serif` (the name before the colon), with the font address kept free of anything after `display=swap`; without the generic fallback a grid drawn before the face is ready falls back to the browser's default serif. Public Sans is loaded once, by the theme; the page CSS imports only Source Serif 4.

### Named Rules
**The One Figure Face Rule.** Every numeral, from the largest total to a digit inside a sentence, a table cell or a chart tick, is Source Serif 4 with tabular lining figures. Never let a number fall back to the sans.

**The Still Column Rule.** Figures are right-aligned to a shared column and tabular, so moving a slider changes digits, never positions.

## Layout

The main column is capped at 1280px with 2.2rem above it; the sidebar is the bookcloth, compact enough that the first assumption slider shows on a laptop screen. The stock and fund pages open with the crumb, the title row (title and chips left, Excel and Share actions right in a 2.4 : 1.6 split), any pad notes, then two columns: the verdict sheet and the footing sheet (on the fund page, "How the model gets there": two cash lines footing to the value per fund share). Tabs follow on one ruled line, labelled Overview, Priced in, Outlook, Forecast, Scenarios, Company (What's inside on a fund). On the stock page the Overview tab holds only the fair-value-by-method chart and, beneath it, the caption with the method count and how the band is drawn; the five-year price-history chart sits on the Outlook tab, after the outlook fan.

A row of figures is one bordered sheet split by hairline dividers rather than several boxes; it becomes a 2 × 2 grid when its container is under 820px. Scenario cards are three plain sheets in a row. The quality scorecard is an auto-fill grid of 250px-minimum cards with a 12px gap. Spacing runs on 4 / 8 / 12 / 16px steps. Below 640px everything stacks to one column (verdict, footing, figures 2 × 2, chart), type steps down as listed above, and a phone-only "Your assumptions" line above the tabs repeats the sidebar's settings with the figures in blue and points to the » toggle. Tabs never shrink or wrap; the row scrolls sideways instead. Charts keep every method at full size on phones.

## Elevation & Depth

The system is flat. Depth comes from a lighter sheet (pad-sheet) laid on the ground (pad-ground) and a 1px rule at its edge; nothing casts a shadow, in either theme (dark mode explicitly strips Streamlit's shadows). Emphasis is carried by ink weight and rules, never by lift.

### Named Rules
**The Hairline Rule.** Separation is a 1px rule or hairline, never a shadow, a gradient or a tinted card.

## Shapes

Three corners only: 4px for tags (chips, badges, metric deltas), 6px for controls (buttons, sidebar menu items), 8px for sheets (panels, charts, tables, quality cards). Tabs are square and marked by a 3px underline sitting on the 1px tab baseline. The one ornamental line in the system is the 4px double rule under a footed total; the one ornamental mark is the pad note's 8px amber dot.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Shape:** gently rounded controls (6px).
- **Default:** Streamlit's bordered button on the pad, label 600 weight; in dark mode filled with night-sheet and bordered with night-rule. Primary buttons at night fill with raised cloth and white text.
- **Disabled:** full opacity, transparent fill, muted-ink label and a dashed border, so it reads as "this one is current" (the scenario card for your own settings) rather than as a faded control. Default cursor.
- **Crumb:** a tertiary "Home" button in ledger green (600, 0.875rem) followed by a muted "›" and the current section.
- **Home index buttons:** the "→ Value a stock", "→ Compare" and other buttons on the Home index are written in ledger green (600), icon included, and underline on hover, since they navigate.
- **Loading a scenario:** the button writes the values into the sliders and a toast confirms what was loaded, since the sliders may be hidden on a phone.

### Chips
- **Style:** 4px tags on the sheet colour with a rule border, small-label type, text #2e4a3d (soft ink at night). The ledger variant (stock or fund type) writes in ledger green edged in chip-ledger-edge; the "you" variant writes in the visitor's blue edged in chip-you-edge.
- **Picked tickers (sidebar):** multiselect tags filled with blue on the cloth and written in deep blue, text and remove icon alike.

### Cards / Containers
- **Corner Style:** 8px.
- **Background:** pad-sheet on pad-ground (night-sheet at night). Sheet styling is applied by container key (verdict, footing, figure rows, cards, and the three scenario cards by their own keys only), so a stray bordered container stays Streamlit's default.
- **Shadow Strategy:** none (see Elevation & Depth).
- **Border:** 1px rule.
- **Internal Padding:** Streamlit's bordered-container default; quality cards 14px 16px.

### Inputs / Fields
- **Style:** Streamlit widgets with borders shown, 8px base radius, filled with pad-tint; on the sidebar, the cloth's deep green with light placeholder text (#b9d0c2).
- **Sliders:** assumption values above the thumb are written in blue on the cloth, Source Serif 4 700, tabular.

### Navigation
- **Sidebar menu:** full-width text buttons on the cloth, left-aligned, nav type in #d5e4da; hover adds a 7% white wash; the current page fills with raised cloth and turns white 700. Focus is a 2px night-you-blue ring.
- **Tabs:** one ruled line that never wraps; each tab is fixed at its natural width (`flex: 0 0 auto`, labels `nowrap`) and the row scrolls sideways with a thin scrollbar when it overflows. Labels 600 0.9rem in muted ink, ink on hover; the selected tab gets a 3px ledger-green underline and ledger-green label. No transition. Focus is a 2px blue outline offset 2px.
- **Expanders:** quiet one-line headings in ledger green (600, 0.875rem) with a book icon, underlined on hover; no box.

### Figure Row
One bordered sheet of metrics separated by hairline dividers. Labels in muted ink (small label), values in the figure face (figure metric; figure compact in compact rows; figure small under 440px). Deltas are 4px tags; differences between two rates are written in percentage points ("+2.1 pts vs. priced in"), never as a percent of a percent. The one neutral delta ("vs. your setting") sits in soft ink on a pale tint.

### Verdict Sheet
A plain sheet whose h3 headline alone carries the result, following the Fifteen Percent Rule: cheap-green when the price sits more than 15% below the estimate, red-ink more than 15% above, plain ink within 15%. Body text and the caption are never coloured. On the stock page the estimate is the methods' middle estimate (their median): the headline says where the price sits against it ("priced well above the middle of its fair-value estimates" past 35%), the first sentence gives today's price and that middle figure, and the second clause gives the spread of the methods and how much to trust it. The method count and how the band is drawn live in the caption under the fair-value chart, not in the verdict. The "Priced in" tab's verdict uses the same sheet and the same rule for how demanding the priced-in growth is. Amber-ink headlines appear only when a model cannot value the company or fund at all.

### Pad Note
A note about how far to trust the numbers is a ruled line on the pad, not a banner: a 1px rule above and below, soft-ink text at 0.9rem / 1.5, indented 22px, with an 8px amber-ink dot in the margin. Never a tinted alert box.

### The Footing (signature)
The computation added up as a column: an uppercase muted caption, then rows of label (body line) and right-aligned figure separated by hairlines, with the operator (+, −, =, ÷) in a fixed 1.1em muted margin. Each line is written in its own unit ($37.76B beside $3.15T, never $0.04T), so small lines stay readable; the figures still right-align. Subtotals are 600 in ink; negatives are red ink in parentheses with a hanging bracket; the total is figure total underlined with a 4px double rule; today's price follows at 1.25rem with no rule.

### Data Tables
Read-only tables (past financials, peers, upside and downside, what-if grids) are rendered as HTML tables on a sheet, set in the Pad Figures stack, scrolling sideways inside their sheet when wide. Headers are muted ink 600. Negative money and percentages are written in accounting brackets ($2.49B) in red ink; signed changes elsewhere carry a plus. Interactive or very long grids remain Streamlit data grids.

### Fair Value by Method Chart
Horizontal range bars per method (34% row height), coloured by source: visitor's blue for the model, ledger green for peers, analyst amber for analysts, the market's range outlined only in muted ink. An ink tick marks each middle estimate, a dashed 2px ink line marks today's price, and a ledger-green wash (8% light, 14% dark) shades the band of middle estimates. The band's label, in muted ink on a sheet-coloured background, depends on its width: "Most methods agree here" only when the band is narrow (top under 1.35× bottom), otherwise "Where the middle estimates fall". Method names sit above each bar with their range on the right, on sheet-coloured backgrounds so the price line passes behind them. Hover notes are wrapped at 30 characters so the label fits a phone-width card. Clicking a bar focuses that method and dims the others to 22%; double-click restores them. No mode bar.

### Price History Chart
On the Outlook tab: the share price over five years in market grey, the visitor's middle estimate as a dashed blue line, and dotted Pessimistic and Optimistic lines in the scenario hue over a 10% blue wash, each dotted line named in muted ink at its right end on a sheet-coloured label.

### Charts (general)
Plotly on the sheet colour, chart-grid gridlines, rule-coloured zero lines, soft-ink text, Pad Figures first in the font stack, a horizontal legend below the plot, x-unified hover, no toolbar. Scenarios share one blue hue that deepens from pessimistic (#8193d6) through yours (#2747b8) to optimistic (#14256b). Growth bars set "Priced in by today's price" (ledger green) against "Your assumption" (blue) and grey reality checks; the Yardstick bar is market grey with a sheet-coloured diagonal hatch, marking it as an average of the others. The backtest scatter shades by year on a ledger-green sequential scale (#dbe6dd to #1e4636; #22362c to #8fd1ad at night).

### Quality Badges
4px tags, 0.75rem 700: Strong in cheap green on badge-strong-tint, OK in badge-ok-ink on badge-ok-tint, Weak in badge-weak-ink on badge-weak-tint, each edged with its matching badge edge; not-applicable in muted ink. At night they take the Night Up / amber / Night Down tints.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do:
- **Do** set every numeral in Source Serif 4 with tabular lining figures, through Pad Figures in front of Public Sans.
- **Do** end every computed answer on a footing: right-aligned figures each in its own unit, operators in the margin, a 4px double-ruled total, today's price beneath.
- **Do** write the visitor's own assumptions, and what is computed from them, in the reserved blue ink (#2747b8 on the pad, #b8c6ff on the cloth, #9db0ff at night), and what the market prices in in ledger green.
- **Do** write negatives in red ink inside parentheses, in footings and tables alike, with the closing bracket hanging outside the column in a footing.
- **Do** separate with 1px rules and hairlines on flat sheets with 4 / 6 / 8px corners.
- **Do** colour only the verdict's h3 headline ink, and leave it plain ink when the price is within 15% of the estimate.
- **Do** write a difference between two rates in "pts".
- **Do** write notes about trusting the numbers as a pad note: a ruled line with a small amber dot.

### Don't:
- **Don't** use shadows, gradient cards, tinted panel backgrounds, tinted banners or star scores.
- **Don't** use blue for anything that is not the visitor's own layer: not brand accents, not market data, not decoration.
- **Don't** use ledger green to signal a judgement; it is the binding, not a verdict.
- **Don't** colour a verdict amber for "in line" or "can't work out"; those are plain ink.
- **Don't** set a number in the sans, or in proportional figures.
- **Don't** fade captions or disabled buttons with opacity (Streamlit's 60% caption fade is overridden to full opacity); give them a colour that passes contrast on its own ground.
- **Don't** let tabs shrink or wrap; let the row scroll.
- **Don't** use amber for anything but analysts, the pad-note dot and the cannot-value caution, or blue, peer green or analyst amber for demand levels.
